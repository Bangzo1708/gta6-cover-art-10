Vice Diagonal is an offline web app that turns your photos and a caption into GTA 6-style cover art. It arranges your images across three diagonal panels under a sunset title band, with three layouts, five colour grades and three title styles, a live editor for cropping, positioning and lettering, and one-click PNG export in 1080 x 1080, 1080 x 1350 and 1920 x 1080.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Everything runs locally — your photos are read on your own machine and never uploaded.
